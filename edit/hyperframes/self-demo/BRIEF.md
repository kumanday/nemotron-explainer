# Composition BRIEF — self-demo (submission film)

## Scope
A 90–120 second explainer (strictly under 180 s) that demonstrates the nemotron-explainer
workflow itself: a real brief, a real Pi + Nemotron 3.5 Super VL (Nebius Token Factory)
run, storyboard and TTV asset handoff, editable HyperFrames motion graphics, real visual
review with a evidenced revision, and the rendered result. The film is produced end-to-end
by this workflow.

## Format
- English, landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4.
- Narration: Media Tooling configured English voice, `eleven_v4`, via `media-tts-frames`
  producing `audio_meta.json`; no music, no SFX.
- Generated footage: 1–2 TTV clips, audio muted, through the reviewed TTV
  request/plan/approval/import/select chain.
- Typography, diagrams and precise copy in HyperFrames; real tool captures supply the
  demonstration.
- Restrained dark visual direction, readable type, small accent palette, titles/captions
  inside safe areas.

## Review mode
review-mode: auto — Nemotron authors and visually reviews actual frame images; bounded
corrections only; publication remains the user's decision. Carried into DECISIONS.md.

## Source of truth
briefs/self-demo.md (creative brief and story beats), PLAN.md (milestones and deadline),
configs/budget.json ($100 cumulative media authorization).

## Delivery
- Editable source: edit/hyperframes/self-demo/
- Final: renders/submission-demo.mp4
- Preview: submission/preview.html
- Evidence: submission/provenance.json, submission/verification.md
