# Project description draft

Nemotron Explainer is a Pi workflow that produces a mixed-media explainer video
with Nemotron 3.5 Super VL on Nebius Token Factory, Media Tooling, HyperFrames
and TTV Pipeline. The film demonstrates the workflow building and reviewing its
own output on a real brief (the Tidepool worked example: one-shot baseline then
loop rounds), with the viewer judging the before and after.

The submission video (`renders/submission-demo-v3.mp4`, 148.87 s, 1080p30,
H.264/AAC) was authored, rendered, blind-reviewed and verified end-to-end
through this workflow. It integrates real TTV-generated footage as a designed
layer, cue-timed explanatory motion graphics, and ElevenLabs eleven_v4
narration. `scripts/check-delivery.py` passes all gates on the final artifact,
and its SHA-256 is bound to `analysis/delivery-evidence.json`.

Track: Best Apps and Agents. Public claims and links are drawn from the
accepted versioned artifact and its verified receipts. Publication awaits the
owner's authorization.
