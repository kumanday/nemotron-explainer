# Composition BRIEF — self-demo-v2 (proof + mixed-media film)

## Scope
Prove the mixed-media capability first: a 15–20 s proof containing one real
TTV-generated clip and at least two explanatory motion sequences, narrated.
Then expand the successful proof into the 90–120 s self-demo film
(renders/submission-demo-v2.mp4). The film is produced end-to-end by the
Nemotron + Media Tooling + TTV + HyperFrames workflow.

## Format
- English, landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4, under 180 s.
- Narration: Media Tooling configured English voice, eleven_v4, via
  media-tts-frames producing audio_meta.json; no music, no SFX.
- Generated footage: 1–2 TTV clips, audio muted, through the reviewed TTV
  request/plan/approval/import/select chain; mounted as timed <video> layers.
- Typography, diagrams and precise copy in HyperFrames; real tool captures
  supply the demonstration.
- Restrained dark visual direction, readable type, small accent palette,
  titles/captions inside safe areas.

## Review mode
review-mode: auto — Nemotron authors and visually reviews actual frame images;
two visual review passes max; real motion/audio checked on short boundary clips.
Recorded in DECISIONS.md.

## Source of truth
briefs/self-demo.md (creative brief and story beats), PLAN.md (milestones and
deadline), configs/budget.json ($100 cumulative media authorization), RECOVER.md
(production requirements).

## Delivery
- Editable source: edit/hyperframes/self-demo-v2/
- Final: renders/submission-demo-v2.mp4
- Evidence: analysis/delivery-evidence.json, edit/hyperframes/self-demo-v2/REVIEW.md
